JARVIS Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Who uses JARVIS

JARVIS is a personal assistant built for and used only by Jared Ramm. It is not available to anyone else.

What Google data JARVIS uses

With Jared's permission, JARVIS accesses his own Gmail account to:

  • read and search his email,
  • create and edit drafts (an email is sent only when Jared asks),
  • organize mail with labels, archive and trash (never permanent delete).

Where the data goes

Email data is processed on Jared's own computer to answer his requests. JARVIS does not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI models. Parts of a message may be sent to the AI service JARVIS uses only to answer Jared's own request.

Google API Services User Data Policy

JARVIS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
